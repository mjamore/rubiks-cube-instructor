Meet your cube

Look around. In Learn, drag the cube to look around. In Solve, drag outside it to look around; swipe a face to turn a layer. In Practice, follow the animated moves on your own cube. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

Give it a twist. Tap a face button below the cube. R means right, U means up, F means front. A prime (′) turns it backward; 2 means turn twice. Clockwise is always as you look straight at that face.

Learn by doing. Choose any topic in Learn to explore pieces, the six sides, and turns. Each topic checks your understanding. Solve also has seven prepared beginner examples.

Solve your own scramble. Open Solve and choose “Solve this cube” for a complete, efficient solution with spoken or written turn instructions. This uses a two-phase solver; it does not produce a layer-by-layer beginner solution.

Keyboard magic. R, L, U, D, F, B turn a side; hold Shift to reverse. In Solve, S mixes it up and Space plays or pauses. In Practice, Space starts or stops the drill.

Bring your cube. Scan all six sides in good light. Always check the colors before starting. Images stay in your browser.
