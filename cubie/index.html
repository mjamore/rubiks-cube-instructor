THE BASICS

Learn the 6 sides

Keep white on top and green in front. Each letter names one face. Tap a move to see it, then try it yourself.

R One clockwise quarter turn

R′ One counterclockwise quarter turn

R2 A half turn (two clicks)

Always imagine looking directly at the face you’re turning. The back and bottom can feel tricky — go slowly.

How to play

Look around. Drag the cube with one finger or your mouse. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

Give it a twist. Tap a face button below the cube. R means right, U means up, F means front. A prime (′) turns it backward; 2 means turn twice. Clockwise is always as you look straight at that face.

Learn by doing. The beginner path has seven practice lessons. Each one starts with a small, prepared example so you can understand the idea.

Solve your own scramble. Choose “Solve my cube” for a complete, efficient solution with spoken or written turn instructions. This uses a two-phase solver; it does not produce a layer-by-layer beginner solution.

Keyboard magic. R, L, U, D, F, B turn a side; hold Shift to reverse. S mixes it up. Space plays or pauses.

Bring your cube. Scan all six sides in good light. Always check the colors before starting. Images stay in your browser.
